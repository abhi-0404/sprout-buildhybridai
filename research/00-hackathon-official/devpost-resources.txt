Google Quota Accounts:
How to request a Google Quota Account
Register for #BuildHybridAI on Devpost.
Submit the Google Quota Account form. Tell us which feature you need credits for.
Pass the eligibility check. Devpost confirms you're registered and eligible under the Official Rules.
Verify your identity. You'll get an email from our third-party verification partner asking for a photo of your ID and a selfie.
Get approved. We'll email you once you're approved and tell you which round you're in.
Receive your account on Thursday. Your sign-in details arrive by email on the Thursday your round starts.
Sign in and build. Open an incognito or private window, sign in with the account, create a project, add Google Firebase to your project, and follow the Firebase AI Logic docs to start your project.
Issues with Quota Accounts
If there are questions or issues with your Google Quota Account please reach out in Discord
