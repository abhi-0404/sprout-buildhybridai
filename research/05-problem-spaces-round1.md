## Key evidence
- **Medical billing errors:** KFF found 43% of adults received a bill they thought had an error, and 51% of them could not resolve it. https://www.kff.org/policy-watch/could-consumer-assistance-be-helpful-to-people-facing-medical-debt/
- **Dementia caregivers:** 11.5-13M unpaid in the US, giving about 19B hours a year, and 59% report high stress. https://www.alz.org/getmedia/c05f7ba4-9aea-4cb0-8898-5e8bff3f0930/executive-summary-2025-alzheimers-disease-facts-and-figures.pdf
- **ASHA health workers:** about 1M in India; poor connectivity and parallel paper registers. https://www.ideasforindia.in/topics/productivity-innovation/is-digitalisation-a-double-edged-sword-for-workers-in-indias-public-healthcare-system
- **Scams:** Indians lost ₹22,845 crore in 2024. However, Google Messages already runs on-device plus cloud scam detection, which is a fatal overlap. https://www.droid-life.com/2026/10/14/google-messages-is-getting-a-massive-gemini-powered-scam-defense-upgrade/
- **Sextortion:** NCMEC logged 50,000+ financial sextortion reports in 2025. https://us.missingkids.org/blog/2026/ncmec-releases-new-sextortion-data-2025
